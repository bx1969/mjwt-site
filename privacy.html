Privacy Policy

Mahjong World Tour ("the app") is a tile-matching travel game for iPhone and iPad, published by Upside Technologies ("we", "us"). Upside Technologies is responsible for the app and for this policy. This policy explains what information is involved when you play and who handles it. In short: we don't run our own servers, don't require an account, and don't sell your information.

Information that stays on your device

Your game progress (cities visited, miles, passport stamps, souvenirs, settings and the traveler name you enter for your passport) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it. It is deleted when you delete the app or choose Reset progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows occasional ads, provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's IP address, device type, app interactions with ads, and approximate location derived from your IP address.

  • We ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before Google can use your device's advertising identifier. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but are not personalized using that identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and Switzerland), you'll see a consent form before any personalized ads are shown.
  • Google's use of this information is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you buy "Remove ads", ad breaks between sights stop.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores may be posted to Game Center leaderboards, where other players can see your Game Center nickname and score. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can manage Game Center in iOS Settings › Game Center.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; we only learn from Apple whether the "Remove ads" purchase is active so the app can turn ads off.

Music and content downloads

Some city music is streamed from a public GitHub repository. As with any web request, GitHub can see your device's IP address when the app downloads a song. No personal information is sent with these requests. See GitHub's privacy statement.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account or collect your name, email or contacts.
  • We don't use our own analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information for money.

Children

Mahjong World Tour is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Your choices and rights

You can limit ad tracking with the iOS Tracking setting, reset your in-game progress at any time, or delete the app to remove all locally stored data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal information held by the services above; for data handled by Google or Apple, contact them directly using the links in this policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
