Common questions

How do I play?

Tap two matching tiles that are free to clear them. A tile is free when nothing sits on top of it and it isn't blocked on both its left and right sides. If you tap a tile that's trapped, it gives a little wiggle. Clear every tile to reveal the sight behind the board.

How do I travel to a new city?

Each city has three sights. After the third, answer the city quiz to collect your passport stamp, then open the map, pick your next destination and fly there with the miles you've earned.

How do souvenirs work?

Clearing a board perfectly, with no mistakes, adds a souvenir slice to that city's prize wheel. You can also choose "Extend stay" once you've finished a city, then clear one more board to take its souvenir home.

I bought "Remove ads" but I still see ads

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. The purchase carries over to your other iPhones and iPads the same way.

Is my progress saved?

Your progress is saved on your device as you play. Deleting the app also deletes its saved progress, so keep the app installed if you want to keep your miles, stamps and souvenirs.

How do I start over?

Open Settings and choose Reset progress. This clears your miles and passport and sends you back to New York.

The music or sound stopped

Check that sound and music are on in the game's Settings and that your iPhone isn't on silent. If the music doesn't return after you switch apps, close the game fully and open it again.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
